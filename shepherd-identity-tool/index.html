Privacy Policy
Last updated: April 18, 2026

What We Collect

When you create an account, we collect your name, email address, and a password (stored securely via hashing). As you use Shepherd Formation, the content you enter — including prophetic words, dreams, Scripture references, spiritual gifts, and journal entries — is stored in your account so you can access it across sessions.

AI-Powered Features

Shepherd Formation includes an AI assistant (“Shepherd Guide”) that helps you explore patterns across your entries. When you interact with the Shepherd Guide, your conversation and relevant profile data are sent to our AI provider (Anthropic) for processing. Anthropic does not use your data to train their models. Conversations with the Shepherd Guide are stored in your account for your reference.

How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely to provide and improve the Shepherd Formation experience. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. We may use anonymized, aggregated usage statistics to improve the platform and guide pastoral teaching.

Pastoral Access

Designated pastoral leadership at Shepherd Church NWI may have administrative access to the platform for support and stewardship purposes. Admins can view aggregate usage statistics and manage platform resources, but your personal journal entries, dreams, and conversations with the Shepherd Guide are protected by row-level security and are not visible to pastors through the admin interface. If you voluntarily share content with a pastor outside the app, normal pastoral confidentiality practices apply.

Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored securely using Supabase, which provides encryption at rest and in transit. Access to your data is protected by row-level security policies — only you can access your own entries through the app.

Your Rights

You can export all of your data at any time using the export feature in the app. You may request deletion of your account and all associated data by contacting a pastor at Shepherd Church NWI. Upon deletion, your data is permanently removed from our systems within 30 days.

Cookies & Local Storage

We use local storage in your browser to save your session and preferences so the app works offline. We do not use third-party tracking cookies or analytics services that track you across the web.

Contact

For questions about this privacy policy or your data, please contact a pastor at Shepherd Church NWI.
